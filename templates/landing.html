The Problem

The "Bookmark Black Hole"

Saving reels takes 1 second. Finding them later takes 5 minutes of infinite scrolling. Here is why native bookmarks fail human memory.

✕

Native Instagram / YouTube Save

Engineered for scrolling, not retrieval

  • Zero Searchability: Cannot search your saved bookmarks by tool name, topic, or keyword.
  • Total Context Loss: Must scrub through a 60-second video just to remember why you saved it.
  • Manual Collections Fail: Creating manual folders on mobile is high-friction and quickly abandoned.
  • Data Siloed Forever: No way to export your saved videos into Notion, Obsidian, or Markdown.
✓

Reelbox AI Second Brain

Engineered for instant information scent

  • Instant Global Search (Ctrl K): Real-time debounced search across titles, summaries, tools, and tags.
  • 3-Second Key Takeaways: Gemini AI extracts 3 bullet points upfront—no video re-watching required.
  • Zero-Touch Auto Folders: AI automatically organizes bookmarks into Tech, Design, Finance, Tools, etc.
  • 1-Click Notion Export: Download your entire library as Notion-ready Markdown (.md) or CSV.
Frictionless Workflow

How Reelbox Works

No new mobile apps to download. Reelbox lives right where you already browse and share.

Step 01

Forward via Telegram

When browsing on Instagram, YouTube Shorts, or TikTok, tap Share → Telegram → @ReelboxxBot. That's it—one single tap from your natural habits.

Step 02

AI Auto-Synthesis in < 3s

Google Gemini AI scrapes the reel metadata, cleans headlines, extracts 3 actionable takeaways, identifies tools, and files it into smart categories.

Step 03

Search & Retrieve in Seconds

Open your private vault on desktop or mobile. Hit Ctrl K to find exact tools, toggle platform filters (Instagram/YouTube/TikTok), or export to Notion.
